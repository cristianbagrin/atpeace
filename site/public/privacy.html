Privacy

At Peace keeps everything on your computer. Unless you sign in to the optional account, nothing is sent anywhere.

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports.
  • No remote code, remote fonts or remote images. Everything the extension shows ships inside it.
  • Without an account the extension makes no network requests of its own. The manifest declares data_collection_permissions: { "required": ["none"] }, which is Firefox's way of saying so.

The optional account

Settings, then Account, lets you sign in with the same account as Chris's other apps (account.cristianbagrin.com), so your settings and presets follow you to every browser. In Firefox, the browser asks first, because signing in sends data (authenticationInfo, personallyIdentifyingInfo in the manifest). If you sign in:

  • Your email and password go to account.cristianbagrin.com to sign you in. The server keeps a salted PBKDF2 hash of the password, never the password itself.
  • Your settings and presets you saved are stored with your account and sent to your other signed-in browsers. The settings lock is never sent, and neither are usage stats or run state.
  • While the settings lock holds, changes from your other browsers wait until you unlock.
  • Sign out stops syncing; your settings stay in the browser. Deleting the account on account.cristianbagrin.com removes what the server keeps, right away.

What it can see

  • youtube.com. The extension runs on www.youtube.com so it can hide parts of the page, follow navigation and apply the behaviors you chose. It reads the page only to do that. Page content is never stored or sent.
  • Other sites. Only if you add a site to Mindful Pause. The browser then asks for permission for that one site, and you can take it back on the browser's extensions page at any time.
  • Browsing history. At Peace does not read it and does not have the permission to.

What is stored, and where

Everything lives in the browser's own extension storage (storage.local) for this add-on, inside your browser profile. Settings and presets are also kept with your account if you signed in (see above).

WhatWhyHow long
Settings (every toggle you changed, the elements you hid, filter words, schedules, the list of pause sites) So your choices survive restarts Until you reset or uninstall
Presets you saved So you can switch between your own setups Until you delete them
Settings lock (if you set one): a mode, and for a password lock a salted PBKDF2 hash So the lock can check a password without storing it Until you remove the lock
Run state (when a Pause session or a focus block ends, extra time you asked for today) So blocks and sessions keep working after a restart Short lived; it only describes what is in force right now
Usage stats: per day, minutes active on a site, how many times it was opened, how many times you turned back or continued, how many times a block stopped you The numbers in the popup and "Your week" in the settings, and the daily limit 90 days, then older days are removed
Install bookkeeping (install time, whether the welcome page was finished, the version) So the welcome page shows once and settings can be upgraded Until you uninstall

Two small things live outside the extension's storage. The Pause breath counter and a redirect loop guard use the page's sessionStorage for the current tab only; both are gone when the tab closes.

How to clear it

  • Usage stats: Settings, then "Your week", then Clear usage history…. It also resets today's count against the daily limit, so it needs an unlock if the settings lock is on.
  • Settings: Settings, then Export / Import / Reset, then Reset…. Presets you saved stay; delete them in the Presets section.
  • Everything: remove the extension. The browser deletes its storage with it.

Backups

Export writes a file with your settings and presets to wherever you save it. At Peace does not read or send that file; treat it like any other file on your disk. It never contains usage stats or the settings lock.
